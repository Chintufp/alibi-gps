Alibi: where am I, really?

GPS is just one witness to where you are, and it can be jammed or faked. Landmarks you aim the camera at, the Sun and stars, (simulated) cell towers, your steps and your own guess are more witnesses. Alibi shows where they agree and catches a lying GPS.

±50 m means you are within 50 m, even if one witness is lying (99 % sure).
